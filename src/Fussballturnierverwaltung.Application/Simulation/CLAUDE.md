# Simulation (Application/Simulation)

Kalibrierungs- und Designwissen der Simulation. Messreihen, Diagnosen und Zahlen stehen in den genannten Issues, nicht hier.

**Querschnittsregeln:** Neue Faktoren rechnen im Normalfall **bitgleich wie vorher und ziehen keinen zusätzlichen Zufall** (je Faktor ein Referenztest). Jede Änderung an einer Konstante würfelt die Zufallsströme neu → Realismus-Messreihe über mehrere Seeds laufen lassen (Skill `realismus-messreihe`), nie den Seed von Hand umstellen; ein schlechter Wert wird nicht aufgeweicht, sondern der Test bekommt ein `Skip` mit Messwert und Issue.

**`SimulationsEngine`** is stateless (pure static methods). It uses the Knuth algorithm for Poisson draws. Pass a seeded `Random` instance for reproducible results. The seed is recorded in `SimulationsLauf.ZufallsSeed`.

**Risk axis and playing style (#673, #667):** in the detailed simulation every team sits on a risk axis (`Spielsituation`: Mauern −2, Defensiv −1, Neutral 0, Offensiv 1, AllesNachVorn 2, Brechstange 3; factors interpolated in between). `Risikoentscheidung` decides which stage is due (leading: Defensiv, then Mauern). `Mannschaft.Grundausrichtung` (5 stages) is the starting point and shifts the switching times by 5 min per stage; `Mannschaft.Flexibilitaet` (3 stages) sets how far a team follows a due stage (`S + (1 − f) · g`) and how fast (window 12/8/4 min) — see `Teamprofil`. Default (ausgeglichen/normal, value 0) behaves exactly as before #667. The seed takes the Grundausrichtung from the ratings (`SpielweisenVorschlag`) plus a hand list (`SpielweiseSeedDaten`).

**`TurnierRunde.Spielbedeutung` (#668):** gering/normal/hoch shifts the starting point of both teams on the risk axis (gering +0.5, hoch −0.25, added to the Grundausrichtung inside `Teamprofil`; switching times unchanged); normal behaves exactly as before. Pre-set by `SpielbedeutungVorbelegung` from the Rundenart (Finale hoch, Spiel um Platz 3 gering) in the round form on every Rundenart change, in the seed templates and once by migration.

**Cards (#682):** `ROTE_KARTEN_JE_90` / `GELBE_KARTEN_JE_90` in `Spielablauf` are per **team** and match (0,075 red = 0,15 per match; 1,88 yellow, Bundesliga 2015/16–2024/25). A red card depends on the match state: `Spielsituation.Rotfaktor` scales the red rate ×1,6 for a team one goal behind and ×0,4 for one one goal ahead (not at larger margins; frequency stays the same). Neither a stronger strength penalty nor the risk axis is the lever (series in the issue).

**Spielstand, Rückzug, Spielkontrolle (#674):** three effects on the chance rate, calibrated against the realism tests: (1) `Spielsituation.Rueckzug` – the **stages** Defensiv/Mauern picked to protect a lead invite pressure (own attack 0,95/0,90, opponent's 1,05/1,10 for a team exactly on the stage); the risk **axis** itself is unchanged. (2) `Spielstandfaktor` – after an equaliser both teams ×0,80, uneven ×0,96, 0:0 unchanged. (3) `Spielkontrolle` – hidden AR(1) state z (×0,97 per minute, stationary σ 0,3), home ×e^z, away ×e^−z, normalised; deliberately **without** goal coupling (momentum measured worse) and without "Nachlassen bei sicherer Führung". It draws from its own stream (`IZufallsabzweigung`): the scripted test randoms (`VorgegebenerZufall`/`VorgegebenerAnfang`) hand it a resting stream, so their per-minute draws stay untouched.
**Schwung nach dem Ausgleich (#692):** (4) `Spielsituation.Schwungfaktor` – whoever equalises has ×1,25 chances until the next goal, the opponent ÷1,25 (`AUSGLEICH_SCHWUNG`); state `_schwung` in `Spielablauf`, set in `TorErzielen` when the goal makes the decisive lead 0 (second leg: aggregate incl. first leg), any other goal ends it. Not applied in extra time; a replay starts a new `Spielablauf` without it. Draws no random number (`AusgleichsschwungOhneAusgleichTests`). Differs from #674's rejected goal coupling: it acts only after an equaliser and ends with the next goal.

**Kondition (#677):** `Mannschaft.Kondition` (Enum schwach −1 / normal 0 / stark 1, Muster `Flexibilitaet`) sets how fast a team tires. `Teamprofil.Ermuedungsfaktor` k = 1,15 / 1 / 0,85; (1) `Kartenfaktor(Ermüdung · k)` per team. (2) `Spielsituation.Konditionsrueckstand` r = (k − 1) · max(0, Ermüdung − 0,5) (zero until half-time, grows in extra time), `Konditionsfaktor` = (1 − A·r_eigen)(1 + B·r_Gegner) on the chance rate (A 0,8, B 1,0; calibrated at `KonditionWirkungTests`; the shared `Zeitfaktor` of extra time stays). (3) `Teamprofil.Offensivdrang` −5 minutes for schwach. **Normal is bit-identical to before #677**, no extra random. Not coupled: Spielkontrolle. Seed: derived once from EA-FC endurance values (`tools/Kondition-EAFC`); the repo holds only the level list `KonditionSeedListe` (top quarter stark, bottom quarter schwach, clubs and national teams separately), no mention of the source in the app; existing data by migration `MannschaftKonditionVorbelegen`.

**Nervenstärke (#669):** `Mannschaft.Nervenstaerke` (Enum schwach −1 / normal 0 / stark 1) shifts the hit rate in the **penalty shoot-out** (`Elfmeterschiessen`, base 73 % + (OffensivRating − 70) · 0,002): stark +2, schwach −2 points, **under pressure ±3** (`Nervenwirkung`, pure function, then clamp 0,5–0,92). Pressure: a regular shot whose miss loses the shoot-out immediately; in sudden death every shot. Deliberately **no general pressure curve**; **normal is bit-identical** and draws no extra random (`NervenstaerkeTests`). Not affected: penalties in play (`Chancenart`, 78,5 %), match simulation, Kondition. Do not punish twice with #679: it acts only in the shoot-out. Seed: from EA-FC values (`tools/Kondition-EAFC`), the repo holds only `NervenstaerkeSeedListe`; existing data by migration `MannschaftNervenstaerkeVorbelegen`; maintained in the team form and the CSV (column `Nervenstaerke`).

**Turnierstärke (#679):** per tournament and team a fixed hidden deviation from strength, drawn once and kept for all games of the tournament. `Turnierstaerke.Wert(staerkeSeed, mannschaftId)` is pure: `Random` from a fixed hash of seed and team id (no `HashCode.Combine`, it differs per process), normal × `STREUUNG` 0,075 (`Normalverteilung`). `Turnierform(Heim, Gast)` carries both values into the engine (optional parameter of `SimulationsEngine.Simulieren`/`…BisWiederholung`/`EntscheidungsverfahrenAnwenden`); factor on the goal expectation `e^(t_Angreifer − t_Verteidiger − σ²)` before the `Clamp`; in a replay the value travels with the team (`Tauschen()`). **Without form (free single game, first/second leg without tournament) bit-identical to before and no extra random.** The shoot-out does not know the form. Storage: `TurnierInstanz.StaerkeSeed` (`int?`), assigned by `ITurnierInstanzRepository.StaerkeSeedSichernAsync`: kept while the tournament has a result; when all results are deleted the next simulation draws a new one; candidate from `Zufallsquelle.SeedZiehen()`, not from the game's `rng`. Master-data ratings stay untouched; Elo (#359) must not read the Turnierstärke. **Measured and rejected:** per-game form of the day, smaller `STAERKE_SPREIZUNG` (details in #679). Effect measurement: `TurnierstaerkeMessung` (`Explicit`).

**Chancenmodell (#431):** `Spielablauf` rolls first a chance per minute (rate `ChancenJe90 · Angriffsfaktor`, probability `r`, always from r ≥ 1; **at most one chance per minute** (#692), a goal acts from the next minute: several chances per minute made their count geometric and over-dispersed the goals), then whose, then the kind (`Chancenart`: penalty 0,16 per team and game at 78,5 %, big chance 1,85 at 37 %, normal chance 10,9 at 7 %; `Chancenmodell`), then the goal by the kind's quota × `Spielsituation.Verwertungsfaktor`. `Chancenprofil` splits the unchanged goal expectation λ from the ratings (midfield = quantity, offence and opposing defence = conversion): `ChancenJe90 · MittlereQuote(Verwertung) = λ`, so calibration #404 and the fast path stay untouched. Only factors acting differently on quantity and quality can change the goal distribution. The risk axis acts separately: more risk = more but worse chances (rate E², quota 1/E); against a team opening up, quantity and quality rise by half each (√G); empty-net goals only count against the all-out attacker's open defence (`Spielsituation.Verwertung.DurchGegner`). `Spielsituation.Zeitfaktor` gives the chance rate a time profile per quarter hour plus stoppage time (extra time unchanged by fatigue). Events: goals carry `SpielEreignis.Chancenart` (old data and everything without a chance `null`); a missed penalty or big chance is `EreignisArt.Grosschance`, a converted in-play penalty a `Tor` ("Tor per Elfmeter"), missed normal chances create no event; `ElfmeterTor`/`ElfmeterVerschossen` belong only to the shoot-out. Set pieces are only the origin in the goal text. Statistic "Großchancen" counts only games with chance data (`SpielErgebnis.MitChancendaten`, set by the simulation). `ABLAUF_KORREKTUR` (seed 678, narrow window; 0,882 since #674) is bound to the sample. Since #683 the realism tests turn red only when a metric exceeds its band by more than 2 jackknife standard errors (noise margin).

**Heimvorteil, Spreizung, Zeitprofil (#694):** `SimulationsEngine.Heimvorteil` (`HEIMVORTEIL` 1,05) multiplies the goal expectation of the team with the venue advantage and divides the opponent's, before `turnierfaktor` and `Clamp`, **not on neutral ground** (with `Spielortvorteil.Auswaerts` the nominal away team has it); master-data home/away strengths stay untouched. It enlarges goal differences, hence `STAERKE_SPREIZUNG` 2,2 → 2,1 (2,0 tips calibration #404). `Spielsituation._zeitprofil` vs. #431: first half ×1,035, second ×0,977 (the tactic additionally moves goals late). Accepted price: goal difference ≥ 3 at the band edge, lower-half winners on neutral ground up (numbers in #694). The home advantage shifts the strength share of the Risikoentscheidung (`_heimAnteil`) by about 2 minutes; the tests with fixed random sequences hold it at 2/3 via `HeimTeam()`/`GastTeam()` (away guest 55 %).

**Dynamische Teamstärke (#359):** switch `TurnierInstanz.DynamischeTeamstaerkeAktiv` ("Dynamische Teamstärke" in the tournament header, default off, cloned); only the switch is visible, no K, no Elo number, no stored history. Internally an Elo model after eloratings (`DynamischeTeamstaerke`, pure): `Elo = 1500 + 18 · (Gesamtrating − 75)`, home bonus 60 (only the team with the venue advantage, 0 on neutral ground), `R_neu = R + K·G·(W − W_e)`, K by category (WM 60, continental 50, cup 30, else 20), G 1 / 1,5 / (11+N)/8. Extra-time goals count, shoot-out and lot are draws, first/second leg and replay singly (the latter with the actual home right). The **state is not stored** but computed before each game from all earlier results (key round, matchday, game number, part – not entry time): a deleted or overwritten result works back by itself. Only the **deviation** from the start value enters the simulation, as a surcharge on the existing `Turnierform` (`WIRKUNG` 0,25 · `VOLLE_WIRKUNG` = `STAERKE_SPREIZUNG`/75/18 per Elo point); the Turnierstärke (#679) does not flow into Elo, no extra random. Wired only in `SimulationsService.VorgangAsync` (per operation and tournament once: strength seed + `DynamischeTeamstaerkeVerlauf`); free single-game/first-second-leg simulation and master data stay untouched, **switch off is bit-identical** (`DynamischeTeamstaerkeAusTests`). Calibrate on the actual update `G·(W − W_e)`, not `W − W_e`. **Known limit:** the bands are measured only for Bundesliga seasons with K 20; at K 30 and above 3/30 seeds were red in the pre-measurement; knockout tournaments are not checked against bands. Switch for the realism series: `REALISMUS_DYNAMIK=1`.

**Tabellenbedarf (#176):** before every league/group/pot-league game (not Swiss system) each team gets exactly one `Tabellenbedarf`, purely from the state **before the matchday** (points, open games, zones of the round from `TabellenZonen`, per group): `TabellenbedarfRechner` works like `RangPrognose` over point bounds without tiebreakers (equal points count as "can overtake"). Precedence: *Ohne Ziel* (zone settled, also mathematically relegated) › *Remis reicht* (a draw secures the best reachable zone) › *Sieg nötig* (after a draw it is lost even with only wins) › *Abstiegskampf* (at most 10 open games, safety not secured, at most 3 points above the first place of a red table-range zone) › *Offen*. A zone under reservation ("the n best thirds") never counts as safe; without zones, without matchday or with an open placeholder in the round the need is *Offen*. Effect: `Teamprofil.Bedarf` shifts the starting point (*Abstiegskampf* −0,25, *Ohne Ziel* +0,5, added to the Spielbedeutung); *Sieg nötig* plays at a draw by the plan for one goal behind, *Remis reicht* by the lead plan, *Ohne Ziel* has no Brechstange (`Risikoentscheidung.Stufenplan`). Wiring: `TabellenbedarfQuelle` loads zones and point system per operation, `TabellenbedarfVerlauf` keeps the state in memory and advances it after each game (`Setzen`, like `DynamischeTeamstaerkeVerlauf`), `SimulationsService` passes `TabellenbedarfJeTeam` as the last optional parameter to `SimulationsEngine.Simulieren` – only for rounds with table and zones. **Knockout, free single game, first/second leg without tournament and need *Offen* are bit-identical to before #176 and draw no extra random** (`TabellenbedarfOhneTabelleTests`). Sizes based on real data Bundesliga 2015/16–2024/25; *Sieg nötig*/*Remis reicht* are not measured against real data. Measurements: `TabellenbedarfMessung`, `TabellenbedarfLaufzeitMessung` (`Explicit`), realism series with `REALISMUS_LAGE=1`. Not part: Mentalität (#684), title/European race as own need, workload (#693).

**Wiederholungsspiel (#547, #691):** part of the `SpielErgebnis`, not a separate game: `HeimToreWiederholung`/`AuswaertsToreWiederholung` (+ `…Verlaengerung`) from the home team's view of the game, `SpielEreignis.ImWiederholungsspiel` marks its events (minutes count from 0 again; the first game's half-time score does not count them). `HeimToreGesamt` stays the first game's score, the winner comes from the replay (`SiegerErmittlung`, also in a knockout second leg without first-leg score). Engine: `WiederholungsspielAusgang` – new `Spielablauf`, home right swapped except on neutral ground or with a host's venue advantage; the extra-time kind *before* the replay in the chain runs again there, the lot etc. only after; at most one replay. Statistics: `MannschaftsSpiel.IstWiederholungsspiel` – the replay is an additional game with its own goals and cards. Input: `ErgebnisEntscheidung.WiederholungAktiv`/`Wiederholung`, fields in the result bar. Regelwerk (#687): `RegelwerkValidator` allows no chain ending with `Wiederholungsspiel` (a level replay would have no winner); existing rule sets with such a chain are not re-checked. Display of the replay (cards, own-step placeholder, `SimulationsEngine.HeimrechtTauscht` shared by engine and display): `src/Fussballturnierverwaltung.WinUi/CLAUDE.md`.
